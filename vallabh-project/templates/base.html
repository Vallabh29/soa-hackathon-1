Refund Destination Closed Account

A standardized demonstration platform designed to streamline refunds when a customer's original bank account, debit card, or digital wallet is permanently closed, frozen, or merged.

Security Notice: Authorized payment providers require rigorous identity matching prior to disbursing refunds to alternative destinations. Never disclose passwords, OTPs, or CVVs.

End-to-End Resolution Workflow

How the system safely captures, validates, and directs refunds from inactive payment destinations

1

Submit Details

The customer submits the original transaction ID, refund amount, closed account details, and specifies an active alternative destination.

2

System Validation

The system verifies that the original destination is confirmed closed/inactive and ensures positive amount and required identity matching.

3

Admin & Provider Review

The request is securely stored in SQLite. Administrators review bank return logs and authorize the replacement payment method.

4

Track & Settlement

The customer monitors progress in real time via their unique Request ID. Once approved, the refund is disbursed to the verified alternate account.

When is Refund Re-Routing Required?

1. Closed Bank Account

When a checking or savings account was closed prior to merchant refund initiation, causing an ACH return code R02.

2. Expired or Cancelled Card

When a debit card, virtual prepaid card, or credit card was reported lost, stolen, or expired with no active replacement.

3. Bank Branch Merger

When routing numbers or IFSC codes change after a banking consolidation, rendering original electronic refund attempts rejected.

Refund Destination Closed Account System

An educational web application demonstrating how fintech platforms and merchants handle refund disbursements when the customer's original receiving bank account or card is permanently closed.

🛡️

Demonstration Safeguards

This is an academic mini-project prototype. It does not connect to live banking networks or payment gateways. It strictly adheres to safety guidelines: no passwords, CVVs, or OTPs are requested or stored, and no automated money movement occurs.

1. The Real-World Problem

Standard electronic payment gateways (such as Stripe, PayPal, Razorpay, or direct merchant acquirers) are designed by default to refund funds strictly to the original source instrument to prevent anti-money laundering (AML) and refund diversion fraud.

However, if the customer closed their bank account, cancelled their card, or their branch was merged prior to the return:

ACH Return Code R02

In US and international direct bank transfers, a closed account triggers an automated return notification back to the originator.

Trapped Merchant Balances

The funds return to the merchant's holding account as unallocated balances, requiring manual customer outreach.

Verification Bottlenecks

Fintech regulations prohibit disbursing refunds to a third party without identity matching and authorized provider verification.

2. How This System Solves It

This portal establishes a standardized, auditable workflow between the customer, the merchant, and payment operations:

  1. Structured Self-Service Form: The customer supplies their transaction ID, confirms the closed account reason, and provides active alternative payment coordinates.
  2. Validation Rules: The system enforces that the account status is explicitly identified as closed. If an active account is selected, the submission is rejected with guidance to wait for the standard refund.
  3. Persistent SQLite Storage: All requests are saved with audit timestamps, customer contact info, and security-sanitized metadata.
  4. Administrative Oversight: Payment operations review return logs, verify destination ownership, and transition the request through Pending → Under Review → Approved → Resolved.
  5. Real-Time Tracking & Guidance: Customers track their Request ID with tailored advice explaining what is occurring at each stage.
